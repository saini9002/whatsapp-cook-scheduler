How it works

  1. Menus rotate independently for each meal.
  2. At 22:30 Asia/Kolkata, tomorrow’s plan is queued (except Saturday).
  3. Your cook visits in the morning and evening, based on your plan.

Green API’s free plan allows 3 chats per instance. Reliability depends on the provider and WhatsApp; this app does not promise delivery.
